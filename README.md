# Wei Jing's Peaceful English Sanctuary & Lesson Notes System

A calm, interactive digital companion and study sanctuary for **Wei Jing's Preply English lessons with Louie**, deployed directly to GitHub Pages at **[https://log210788.github.io/wt/](https://log210788.github.io/wt/)**.

---

## Interactive Features in this Repository

1. **[Wei Jing's Lesson 01 Notes (index.html)](https://log210788.github.io/wt/)**:
   - Complete 50-minute cleaned transcript (`07:12` to `57:03`) across 6 thematic chapters.
   - **55 inline flagged B2-C2 vocabulary items**, workplace collocations, and idioms with British English (`en-GB`) audio pronunciation, phonetic IPA, exact timestamps, and traditional/simplified Chinese context.
   - Dual-mode inspection: hover tooltips via Tippy.js plus a sticky **Vocabulary Spotlight** side panel.
   - **16 automated note-taker spelling fixes** sorted out cleanly (e.g. *Shrek* misheard as *"the shrimp"*, *tick-tock* as *"TikTok"*, *commands* as *"comments"*, and *bat* as *"bed"*).
   - Celebration of **6 natural student expressions**, including Wei Jing's brilliant upward persuasion strategy connecting the pizza party to the US Vice President's visit!
   - 6 gentle, encouraging grammar and phrasing refinements.
   - 4 editorial deep-dives into executive psychology (*the crocodile brain*), technical mentorship (*Linux VMs and Docker*), management autonomy (*"die by the sword"*), and British folklore (*theatrical origins of "break a leg"*).
   - Interactive 12-card active recall flashcard deck, 6-question context quiz with confetti, and 3 quiet reflection prompts for next lesson.
   - Calming London autumn breeze canvas animation and Web Audio API procedural soundscape synthesizer (Kensington Breeze, Soft London Rain, Warm Study Hearth).

2. **[Automated Speech-to-Text Corrections (ASR Fixes)](https://log210788.github.io/wt/)**:
   - Transparently logs every automated transcriber mistake so the student knows their notes are spotless and error-free.
